# Emberheart

PROMPT: Kingdom-Building Arc – Emberheart

Begin kingdom blueprint arc.

Kaelrath Emberhide — The Flamekeeper, God-Ascendant of Emberheart — now presides over a golden age of growth. With divine threats quelled, the council unified, and the people exalted in morale, your focus shifts toward refinement, expansion, and peace.

This is a peaceful, kingdom-sim arc with no active wars or divine calamities. The goal is to guide Emberheart through an era of prosperity by building infrastructure, managing noble houses, expanding magical and mundane services, and fostering the next generation of legends.

DIVINE BLOODLINE MECHANICS

Summary: Kaelrath’s children and heirs are touched by divinity — each manifests traits from flame, shard, or memory-based domains. Not all are equal, and their power scales with age, trials faced, and proximity to divine flame.

Types of Inheritance:

Flame-Touched: Enhanced reflexes, heat immunity, or charisma. Passive aura presence.

Shard-Woven: One minor magical ability tied to a shard Kaelrath has mastered.

Dream-Blooded: May possess dreamwalking, memory-sight, or subconscious prophecy.

Echo-Bound: Rare. Attuned to one of Kaelrath’s past actions; may act as divine echoes themselves.

Ascension Paths:

Any child of Kaelrath may attempt a Flamekeeper Trial when they come of age.

Completion grants unique relics, divine sparks, or god-chosen destinies.

Wives may also bear “blessed twins” — rare siblings with mirrored abilities.

Known Mechanics:

Cannot be fully killed unless forgotten (memory = life).

Bloodline may awaken further powers when near divine relics or battlefields.

Ritual of Binding allows heirs to share in Kaelrath’s flame under pact terms.

Myth says: The flame does not burn alone. It breeds fire.

THE FORGE ACADEMY

Official Title: The Emberheart Academy of Flame and Steel

Purpose:

A hybrid institution focused on flamecrafting, relic forging, and battle-wrought artisanry.

Personally overseen by Kaelrath for elite apprentices or god-marked youth.

Divisions:

1. Forgebound Track – Weapon and armor specialists, often blessed in Kaelrath’s presence.
2. Memory Smiths – Use memory ore to forge enchanted objects.
3. Divine Flame Studies – Theory and practice of crafting with divine echoes.
4. Fieldwrights – Combat engineers trained to design golem modules and siege rigs.

Key NPCs:

Master Artor – Senior smith, veteran of the Sleeper War.

Apprentice Velin – Young prodigy bonded to memory ore.

Overseer Glenda – Manages student selection and housing.

Special Perks:

Divine blacksmithing lessons once per season.

Access to the inner Vault ore (under permission only).

TRADE ROUTES AND CONNECTIONS

Main Trade Corridors:

1. Flameway Northroad – Connects Emberheart to mountain settlements and iron-rich towns. Used for ore, metals, wyvern-hide, and scholars.
2. Silkspire Trail – Leads to the Fae-Craft borderlands and inkroot groves. Exports dreamglass, divine reagents. Imports magic inks, spices.
3. Verdant Cross – Passes through druid lands. Major logging and alchemy resource line. Used by House Maurellien.
4. Emberlake Waterway – Connects Emberheart to southern coastal ports. Naval supply, trade ships, foreign emissary travel.
5. Ashwake Caravan Spine – Overland supply road fortified against shardborn raids. Controlled in part by House Zevrix and caravan masters.

Trade Notes:

Trade law codex being updated by House Zevrix.

Fae-Craft merchants require dreambinding contracts for major deals.

Magical tariffs imposed for any items emitting residual shard-energy.
