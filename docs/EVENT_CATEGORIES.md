# EmberHeart: Origins — Event Generator Categories

The `EventGenerator` runs periodically and posts updates to the `KINGDOM_HERALD` channel (via the Flame Chronicler). It uses `SETTLEMENT_STATE.json` to determine tone. 

The LLM prompt is instructed to randomly draw from one of the following kingdom-scale event categories.

## 1. Noble Proposals & Trade Disputes
**Focus:** Politics, economy, and the 5 Noble Houses.
- *House Zevrix announces a monopoly on Silkspire dreamglass.*
- *The Gilded Scale Consortium demands lower tariffs at the Obsidian Gate.*
- *A dispute over water rights in Bloomshade halts farming.*

## 2. Divine Omens & The Flame
**Focus:** Kaelrath's domain, the Vault of Seven, divine mystery.
- *The deep Vault of Seven rings with an unearthly bell tone, heard across the Citadel.*
- *A citizen in the Wyrmshadow Commons spontaneously manifests minor healing flames.*
- *The Chronicler notes a shift in the leylines beneath Prismspire Heights.*

## 3. Festivals & Civic Life
**Focus:** Positive morale, district culture.
- *Preparations for the Shard-Day Tournament begin in the Iron March.*
- *Brida's district-wide baking contest causes a run on Flamewheat prices.*
- *The Fae-Craft Borderlands celebrate the Bloom-Moon.*

## 4. Military & External Threats
**Focus:** Defense, the borders, Captain Rynn, General Varka.
- *Wyrmwing scouts report a massing of corrupted shard-beasts on the Ashwake Spine.*
- *Commander Thera requests the royal authority to sink unmarked vessels on Emberlake.*
- *A golem test-firing in the Iron March accidentally levels an abandoned watchtower.*

## 5. Academy & Arcane Incidents
**Focus:** Prismspire, Library of Echoes, Forge Academy, Thalos, Sybelle.
- *An experimental memory-forged blade becomes sentient and refuses to be held.*
- *A novice illusionist traps themselves in a time-dilated bubble.*
- *Sybelle locks down the Crystal Archive due to a 'memory leak'.*

## 6. The Royal Bloodline
**Focus:** The 9 Children, Kaelrath's wives.
- *Ashryn is seen performing the forbidden Fire Dance in the outer rings.*
- *Young Roan's nightmares cause local tremors in a rustic village.*
- *Silviea's illusions are mistaken for genuine divine omens by the populace.*
